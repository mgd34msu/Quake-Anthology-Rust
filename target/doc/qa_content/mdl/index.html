Quake alias model (MDL v6) parser.

Donor provenance: parseMdl in src/formats/q12-model/mdl.ts, with shared readers from src/formats/q12-model/common.ts (see crate::common) and normals from crate::normals.

Skins and decoded vertices are owned; the table of contents is read sequentially, borrowing nothing past validation.
