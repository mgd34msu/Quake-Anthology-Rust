Crate qa_content 

Source
Expand description

Content foundation: resource paths, VFS-adjacent helpers, and Quake format decoders (BSP maps, MDL/MD2/SPR models, WAD archives).

Donor provenance: src/content plus src/formats (except src/formats/images, which stays deferred).

Quake64 (Q1 magic 0x51363420) classifies as BspKind::Q1 but stays rejected by the Q1 reader: packed lighting and BSPX extensions are out of scope, as before.
