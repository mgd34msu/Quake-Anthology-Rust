Module md2 

Source
Expand description

Quake II alias model (MD2 v8) parser with GL commands and animation.

Donor provenance: parseMd2 and decodeMd2Commands in src/formats/q12-model/md2.ts, with shared readers from src/formats/q12-model/common.ts (see crate::common), normals from crate::normals, and pose sampling, interpolation, and geometry builders from src/formats/q12-model/animation.ts.

Skins and decoded vertices are owned; sections are read sequentially through sub-readers, borrowing nothing past validation.
