Expand description

Quake III BSP map (IBSP v46, with v44 normalization) parser.

Donor provenance: parseQ3Bsp in src/formats/q3-map/decode.ts, the entity tokenizer in src/formats/q3-map/entities.ts, the v44 layout decoder in src/formats/q3-map/ibsp44.ts, and the scene adapter in src/formats/q3-map/world.ts.

Unlike the Q1/Q2 readers, lightmap and visibility payloads are owned: IBSP44 normalization rebuilds the file, so borrowed slices could not outlive the call. Decoded records are owned.
